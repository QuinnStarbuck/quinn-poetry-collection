The Truth

by: Quinn Starbuck

the closet needs to be cleaned just for another one to get messy the shelves will be empty waiting for a new family it held me and comforted me but there's an ache in my heart with my head resting on the pillow my head in the clouds i tell my friends goodbye trying not to cry with the house so empty it somehow feels so full we packed everything up but somehow left the most important things behind we say it's exciting we say it's fun but if you look in my eyes you'll see the truth doesn't lie we say we'll remember we promise we won't change but distance has a funny way of making things feel strange i'll miss the little things i never thought i'll miss the creak beneath my feet the doorway i once kissed deep in my soul down in my heart the truth seeps through and my tears ruin the art

The Goodbye I Never Got

by: Quinn Starbuck

everyone says goodbye like it's one moment but sometimes it's a thousand little memories you don't realize until they're gone.

After Dark

by: Quinn Starbuck

i used to think that when people went to sleep the animals would wake up maybe they had little houses hidden underground with tiny beds and little kitchens and whole secret lives i imagined them going about their day while we were dreaming and the whole world kept living while i was asleep i don't know when i stopped wondering when i stopped believing there was something magical happening when nobody was watching maybe it was a secret world that only opened after dark for the misunderstood and the animals soft, beating hearts but sometimes when the night is quiet and my thoughts are loud i like to think they are still there sleeping safely underground.

Time Heals All Wounds

by: Quinn Starbuck

i'm sure you've heard the saying “Time Heals All Wounds” but i'm not sure that's true time doesn't erase memories it just makes them older the wound may close but that doesn't mean you can't see the scar you learn to smile again but you don't forget why you cried you think the wound is healed but then something reminds you and still, you keep laughing you keep living so maybe time doesn't heal all wounds maybe it just gives us enough time to learn how to live with the ones that never fully healed

The Past Is in the Past

by: Quinn Starbuck

i'm sure you've heard the saying “The Past is in the Past” but sometimes i wonder if that's really true cause how can something be so far behind you but still feel so close? sometimes all it takes is an old picture a familiar song or a place i haven't seen in years and suddenly i'm there back when things were different back when everything was the same and i thought nothing could change maybe the past doesn't follow us maybe we're the ones that carry it with us in the things we remember the people we become and the little pieces of ourselves we never really left behind so maybe the past is in the past but that doesn't mean it stopped being a part of us

You Can't Go Home Anymore

by: Quinn Starbuck

i'm sure you've heard the saying “You Can't Go Home Anymore” but what does that really mean? cause sometimes you can go back to the same house same street same room but somehow it still feels so different maybe the place didn't change maybe i did the walls are still there the floors still creak the windows still let in the same sunlight everything is the same so, why does it feel so strange? but i'm not the same person who used to just stand here maybe home isn't just somewhere you can return to maybe sometimes you leave a place without realizing that you're leaving that version of yourself behind

You Don't Know What You Have Until It's Gone

by: Quinn Starbuck

maybe we knew all along maybe we just thought we'd have it forever the sound of laughing in the room over the shoes scattered by the door that nobody thought twice about the sunlight on the kitchen floor the hot, summer days the christmas mornings with everyone gathered on the carpet floor we never say goodbye to ordinary days we assume every day will look like today you don't notice something disappearing when it leaves a little at a time but one day when you look around and realize nothing is exactly the same maybe we knew it was special maybe we just thought special things lasted forever maybe we don't forget what we had maybe we just forget that someday we won't have it anymore
